EMBRACE YOURSELF

Bab 6 - English for Nusantara Kelas 8 📚💪

📖 Reading Material

Embracing yourself means accepting who you are, including your strengths and weaknesses. Everyone is unique and special in their own way!

🤔 Did You Know?

The phrase "Be yourself; everyone else is already taken" is attributed to Oscar Wilde. It reminds us that being unique is a gift!

💪

Self-Confidence

Ways to build self-confidence:

  • Accept your strengths and weaknesses
  • Set goals and work towards them
  • Be positive about yourself
  • Learn from mistakes
❤️

Self-Love

How to love yourself:

  • Take care of your body and mind
  • Be kind to yourself
  • Celebrate your achievements
  • Surround yourself with positive people

📝 Positive Affirmations:

  • "I am enough." — Aku sudah cukup.
  • "I believe in myself." — Aku percaya pada diriku.
  • "I can do it!" — Aku bisa melakukannya!
  • "I am proud of who I am." — Aku bangga dengan diriku.
  • "Every day I get better." — Setiap hari aku semakin baik.

🗣️ Dialogue

👧 Dina:
I'm not good at anything. I feel so useless.
🧑 Reza:
That's not true, Dina! You're great at drawing. Your artwork is amazing!
👧 Dina:
Really? You think so?
🧑 Reza:
Of course! Everyone has their own strengths. You should embrace yourself.
👧 Dina:
You're right. I should be more confident. Thank you, Reza!
🧑 Reza:
Remember, you are unique and special. Believe in yourself!

✏️ Grammar Focus

Expressing Ability (Can, Could, Be able to)

💪

Can (Present)

  • "I can draw."
  • "She can sing."
  • "They can dance."
📖

Could (Past)

  • "I could swim when I was 5."
  • "She could read at age 4."

📝 Positive Self-Expressions:

  • "I can do it!" — Aku bisa!
  • "I am able to succeed." — Aku mampu berhasil.
  • "I believe I can." — Aku percaya aku bisa.
