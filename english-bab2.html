CELEBRATING INDEPENDENCE DAY

Bab 2 - English for Nusantara Kelas 8 📚🎉

📖 Reading Material

Indonesia celebrates its Independence Day on August 17th every year. This special day is called "Hari Kemerdekaan" in Indonesian.

🤔 Did You Know?

Indonesia declared its independence on August 17, 1945. The proclamation was read by Soekarno and Mohammad Hatta in Jakarta.

🏁

Traditional Games

During Independence Day celebrations, people play traditional games:

  • Panjat Pinang — Climbing a greased pole
  • Balap Karung — Sack race
  • Tarik Tambang — Tug of war
  • Makan Kerupuk — Cracker eating contest
🎊

Celebrations

How Indonesians celebrate Independence Day:

  • Flag ceremony — Raising the Indonesian flag
  • Parades — Marching through the streets
  • Decorations — Red and white decorations
  • Competitions — Various games and contests

📝 Key Phrases for Independence Day:

  • "Happy Independence Day!" — Selamat Hari Kemerdekaan!
  • "Long live Indonesia!" — Hidup Indonesia!
  • "We are proud to be Indonesian." — Kami bangga menjadi orang Indonesia.
  • "Let's celebrate together!" — Ayo rayakan bersama!

🗣️ Dialogue

Read this dialogue about Independence Day celebration:

🧑 Andi:
Hey Lisa! Are you excited about the Independence Day celebration tomorrow?
👧 Lisa:
Yes, I am! I can't wait to join the traditional games. What about you?
🧑 Andi:
I'm going to participate in the sack race. I practiced a lot!
👧 Lisa:
That's great! I want to watch the flag ceremony first. It's always so patriotic.
🧑 Andi:
Me too. I feel so proud to be Indonesian on this special day.
👧 Lisa:
Happy Independence Day, Andi! Long live Indonesia!
🧑 Andi:
Happy Independence Day, Lisa! Merdeka!

✏️ Grammar Focus

Expressing Pride and Congratulations

🎉

Congratulations

Use these expressions to congratulate:

  • "Congratulations!"
  • "Happy Independence Day!"
  • "I'm so proud of you!"
  • "Well done!"
💪

Expressing Pride

Use these to express pride:

  • "I'm proud to be Indonesian."
  • "We should be proud of our heroes."
  • "It makes me feel proud."
  • "I'm honored to celebrate this day."

📝 Pattern: "I'm proud to + verb"

  • I'm proud to be Indonesian.
  • I'm proud to celebrate Independence Day.
  • I'm proud to honor our heroes.
