KINDNESS BEGIN WITH ME

Bab 3 - English for Nusantara Kelas 8 📚🤝

📖 Reading Material

Kindness means being friendly, generous, and considerate to other people. It starts with small actions that we do every day.

🤔 Did You Know?

Studies show that being kind releases serotonin and endorphins in the brain, which makes both the giver and receiver feel happier!

🗣️

Kind Words

Words that show kindness:

  • "Thank you" — Terima kasih
  • "Please" — Tolong / Silakan
  • "You're welcome" — Sama-sama
  • "I'm sorry" — Maaf
  • "Excuse me" — Permisi
🤝

Kind Actions

Things we can do to show kindness:

  • Helping someone who is in need
  • Sharing food or things with friends
  • Listening to someone who is sad
  • Smiling at people around you
  • Respecting others' feelings

📝 Example Sentences:

  • "Can I help you?" — Bolehkah aku membantumu?
  • "Would you like some water?" — Maukah kamu minum air?
  • "You did a great job!" — Kamu melakukan pekerjaan yang bagus!
  • "I understand how you feel." — Aku mengerti perasaanmu.

🗣️ Dialogue

🧑 Budi:
Hi Maya! You look sad. What happened?
👧 Maya:
I lost my wallet. I can't find it anywhere.
🧑 Budi:
I'm sorry to hear that. Let me help you look for it.
👧 Maya:
Thank you so much, Budi. You're so kind.
🧑 Budi:
Don't worry. Kindness begins with me. Let's check the classroom.
👧 Maya:
I found it! It was under my desk. Thank you for helping me!
🧑 Budi:
You're welcome! I'm glad I could help.

✏️ Grammar Focus

Offering Help and Expressing Gratitude

🤝

Offering Help

  • "Can I help you?"
  • "Let me help you."
  • "Would you like some help?"
  • "Do you need any assistance?"
🙏

Expressing Gratitude

  • "Thank you very much."
  • "I really appreciate it."
  • "That's very kind of you."
  • "I'm so grateful."
