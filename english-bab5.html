NO LITTERING

Bab 5 - English for Nusantara Kelas 8 📚🚫

📖 Reading Material

Littering is throwing garbage on the ground in public places. It is a bad habit that damages our environment. We must stop littering!

🤔 Did You Know?

Indonesia produces about 6.8 million tons of plastic waste every year. Much of it ends up in the ocean, harming marine animals!

🚫

Prohibitions

Things we must NOT do:

  • "Don't litter!" — Jangan buang sampah!
  • "No littering!" — Dilarang membuang sampah!
  • "Don't throw garbage here!"
  • "Keep off the grass!"
⚠️

Warnings

Warning expressions:

  • "Be careful!" — Hati-hati!
  • "Watch out!" — Awas!
  • "Warning!" — Peringatan!
  • "Danger!" — Bahaya!

📝 Signs and Their Meanings:

  • 🚯 No Littering — Dilarang buang sampah
  • 🚭 No Smoking — Dilarang merokok
  • 🔇 Keep Quiet — Jaga ketenangan
  • ⛔ No Entry — Dilarang masuk

🪧 Public Signs

Learn to read and understand public signs in English:

🚫

Prohibition Signs

  • No Littering = Dilarang buang sampah
  • No Smoking = Dilarang merokok
  • No Parking = Dilarang parkir
  • No Swimming = Dilarang berenang
⚠️

Warning Signs

  • Wet Floor = Lantai licin
  • High Voltage = Bahaya listrik
  • Slippery Road = Jalan licin
  • Danger = Bahaya

✏️ Grammar Focus

Prohibition with "Don't" and "No"

🚫

Using "Don't"

Structure: Don't + Verb

  • Don't litter!
  • Don't smoke here!
  • Don't make noise!
⛔

Using "No"

Structure: No + Noun/Verb-ing

  • No Littering!
  • No Smoking!
  • No Swimming!
