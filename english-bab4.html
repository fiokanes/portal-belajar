LOVE OUR WORLD

Bab 4 - English for Nusantara Kelas 8 📚🌿

📖 Reading Material

Our world is a beautiful place. We must take care of it for future generations. Environmental conservation is everyone's responsibility.

🤔 Did You Know?

Indonesia has the third largest tropical forest in the world! It's home to many unique animals like orangutans, Sumatran tigers, and komodo dragons.

♻️

Reduce, Reuse, Recycle

The 3 R's to save the environment:

  • Reduce — Use less, save resources
  • Reuse — Use things again
  • Recycle — Make new from old
🌳

Protect Nature

Ways to protect our nature:

  • Plant trees — Clean the air
  • Save water — Don't waste water
  • Save energy — Turn off lights
  • Don't litter — Keep clean

📝 Environmental Phrases:

  • "Save the earth!" — Selamatkan bumi!
  • "Go green!" — Hidup hijau!
  • "Reduce, Reuse, Recycle!"
  • "Every day is Earth Day."

🗣️ Dialogue

👧 Sari:
Rudi, why is there so much trash on the river?
🧑 Rudi:
Some people throw garbage into the river. It's very sad.
👧 Sari:
We should do something to help. What can we do?
🧑 Rudi:
We can join the river cleanup this Saturday. Would you like to come?
👧 Sari:
Yes, I'd love to! We must protect our environment.
🧑 Rudi:
Exactly! If we love our world, we must take care of it.

✏️ Grammar Focus

Expressing Obligation (Must, Should, Have to)

💪

Must

Strong obligation:

  • "We must protect the environment."
  • "You must not litter."
💡

Should

Advice or recommendation:

  • "We should recycle plastic."
  • "You should save water."
